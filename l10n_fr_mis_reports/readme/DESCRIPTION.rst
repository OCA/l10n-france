This modules provides MIS Builder Report templates for the French
P&L and Balance Sheet taking the liasse fiscale (`form 2050 <https://www.impots.gouv.fr/formulaire/2050-liasse/liasse-fiscale-du-regime-reel-normal-en-matiere-de-bic-et-dis>`_) and liasse fiscale simplifiée (`form 2033 <https://www.impots.gouv.fr/formulaire/2033-sd/liasse-bicsi-regime-rsi-tableaux-ndeg-2033-sd-2033-g-sd>`_)
as reference.

To know the correspondance between the French P&L and Balance Sheet and the accounts, the source of information is the `Recueil des normes comptables françaises - Comptes annuels <https://www.anc.gouv.fr/files/anc/files/1_Normes_fran%C3%A7aises/Reglements/Recueils/PCG_Janvier2025/Recueil-NF-Janvier-2025.pdf>`_ on the website of the *Autorité des Normes Comptables*, page 432 to 449 (the PDF document has 666 pages).
